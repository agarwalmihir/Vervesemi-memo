1Summary

↑ Top

Vervesemi Microelectronics is a fabless precision analog and mixed-signal semiconductor company founded in 2017 in Noida. It develops chips that translate physical measurements into usable electronic data and licenses reusable semiconductor circuit designs.

The company raised $10 million in Series A funding, announced in February 2026. Reported FY25 total revenue was ₹16.4 crore and net profit was ₹3.39 crore. The company has disclosed fabricated and tested chip programmes, but paid volume production and the revenue split among products, licensing, engineering fees and other income are not publicly established.

Core analytical question. Can an established, profitable engineering and IP business expand into repeatable, high-margin chip product sales at an attractive entry valuation?

11Investment evaluation

↑ Top

Business Model Canvas

Value proposition

Precision analog/ mixed-signal ICs and reusable IP. Potential accuracy, power or integration improvements; independent benchmarks are not public.

Customer segment

Electronics OEMs, chip/ system designers, energy-meter, industrial-control, mobility and avionics equipment companies.

Customer channel

Direct OEM/ design-house sales, foundry IP ecosystems, technical engagement and samples. Distributor arrangements are unverified.

Customer relationships

Long engineering and qualification cycles, customised integration support, and potentially repeat licensing or supply agreements.

Key activities

Analog IC/ IP design, verification, tape-out, testing, qualification, customer engineering and product support.

Key resources

Specialist engineers, reusable IP, patents, silicon designs, EDA tools and customer-specific know-how.

Key partnerships

External foundries and test providers; reported Samsung SAFE and UMC IP ecosystems; government design programmes.

Revenues

Disclosed total and operating revenue. Potential IP licences, non-recurring engineering fees, royalties and chip sales; actual mix is unknown.

Costs

Staff and R&D, EDA licensing, mask/ tape-out costs, wafers, packaging, testing, qualification, sales and working capital.

Decision framework

Four of the six screening gates are assessed as met, while commercial proof and the funded operating path remain partial. This supports deeper review of commercial sustainability and financing economics, rather than a categorical investment conclusion.

Essential open questions. The chip/ IP/ services revenue breakdown, recurring paying customer evidence, independent product economics, collections quality and actual cash runway need to be validated. The database-indicated $49.1 million post-money valuation should be checked against final financing documents.

Short rationale. Experienced founders, fabricated silicon, existing revenue and institutional financing are positive signals. Unverified recurring chip revenue and incomplete cash-flow visibility remain material gaps.
